From backend/endpoints/sockets/scan.py: `_enrich_pc_dlc_from_igdb(rom, component)` returns immediately when the gate returns `None`; it calls `fetch_validated_steam_dlc(rom, candidate)` only after applying a non-None hydrated IGDB candidate.

From the Phase 18 decision D-05: only one hydrated IGDB-resolved DLC identity may trigger Steam DLC lookup; ambiguity and invalid outcomes preserve existing component state.

</behavior>

Do not add fallback name matching, retries, component allocation, or direct component writes. The failure remains non-fatal, but it fails closed for identity authority.</implementation>
</feature>
