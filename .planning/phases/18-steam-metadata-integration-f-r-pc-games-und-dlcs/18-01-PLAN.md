## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID       | Category               | Component         | Disposition | Mitigation Plan                                                                                                                   |
| --------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| T-18-02         | Denial of service      | Steam transport   | mitigate    | Reuse rate limiter, timeout, bounded attempts and 429 backoff; degrade to empty match.                                            |
| T-18-03         | Information disclosure | provider logs     | mitigate    | Log only provider, platform, normalized query, App ID, language, confidence, and fallback outcome, never payloads or secrets.     |
